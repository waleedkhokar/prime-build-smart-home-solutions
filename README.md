## 🚀 About This Project

**Prime Build** is a home services team working in Rawalpindi & Islamabad since **2018** — with **250+ projects**, **98% happy clients**, **50+ experts**, and **24/7 support**.

This website was redesigned from a legacy React app into a **fully modern Next.js 15 application**. It looks and feels like a real web app — with live 3D, scroll-driven stories, and animations that stay fast on phones.

The site has **Home**, **About**, and **9 full service pages** with real work galleries and one-tap contact.

**Services:** Plumbing · Electrical · Tiles & Marble · Kitchen Setup · Washroom Fitting · Carpentry · Paint & Polish · Interior Work · Custom Solutions

## ✨ What Makes It Modern

This project demonstrates modern web development with production-ready Next.js + TypeScript, professional UI/UX using Tailwind CSS, interactive GSAP animations, SEO-friendly architecture, and real-world business application development.

- ✅ **Cinematic Hero** — bold title reveals line by line, scroll expands the panel to full screen
- ✅ **Live 3D Kitchen** — a real Three.js scene builds a full kitchen in 15 seconds, loops only when visible
- ✅ **Walking-Man Story** — scroll and a man walks across the screen, stats and values open step by step
- ✅ **9 Full Service Pages** — cards open inline, every service has its own page with gallery and checklist
- ✅ **Smart WhatsApp Contact** — floating button on every page, service name auto-added on service pages
- ✅ **Fast by Design** — static generation, lazy images, code-split 3D, and reduced-motion support
